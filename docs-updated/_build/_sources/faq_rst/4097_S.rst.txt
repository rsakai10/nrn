Extending Single-Compartment to Multi-Compartment Models
=========================================================

When extending a single-compartment Hodgkin-Huxley (HH) model to a multi-compartmental model, it is common to insert appropriate mechanisms into different neuronal sections. For example:

.. code-block:: hoc

    // Soma and axon
    insert hh
    Ra = 100
    cm = 1

    // Apical dendrites
    insert pas
    Ra = 150
    cm = 1

    // Basilar dendrites
    insert pas
    Ra = 150
    cm = 1

.. note::

   - :attr:`Ra` (axial resistance) and :attr:`cm` (membrane capacitance) are *section attributes*, not mechanisms, representing passive electrical properties of the neurite.
   - The :mod:`pas` mechanism models a passive leak current, often used in dendrites to reflect their resting conductance.
   - Differences in resting potentials between sections with :mod:`hh` and those with :mod:`pas` can cause non-uniform resting membrane potentials; adjusting parameters such as :attr:`e_pas` helps maintain physiological consistency.
   - Adding a :class:`IClamp` or other point process allows current injection for stimulation.
