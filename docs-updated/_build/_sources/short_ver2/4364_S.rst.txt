.. rubric:: Units and Accessibility of hh Mechanism Ionic Currents

The variables ``ina`` and ``ik`` in the Hodgkin–Huxley (:mod:`hh`) mechanism represent the sodium and potassium transmembrane current densities, respectively. Their units are current per membrane area, specifically **mA/cm²**.

.. note::
   Although the :class:`hh` mechanism internally computes and writes ``ina`` and ``ik``, these variables are *not* declared as :keyword:`RANGE` and thus are **not accessible** as attributes of the mechanism instance in either HOC or Python. For example::

       >>> axon = h.Section(name='axon')
       >>> axon.insert('hh')
       >>> h.finitialize(-65)
       >>> axon(0.5).hh.ina
       Traceback (most recent call last):
         ...
       AttributeError: 'nrn.Mechanism' object has no attribute 'ina'

In contrast, the leak current ``il`` *is* :keyword:`NONSPECIFIC_CURRENT` and declared as :keyword:`RANGE`, making it accessible::

    >>> axon(0.5).hh.il
    -0.00321

To obtain sodium or potassium current densities at a segment, use the section segment's attributes ``ina`` and ``ik`` directly, which provide the total ionic current densities in mA/cm²:: 

    >>> axon(0.5).ina
    -0.00122
    >>> axon(0.5).ik
    0.00440

These current densities are typically used to compute ionic concentration dynamics and are therefore exposed at the segment level.
