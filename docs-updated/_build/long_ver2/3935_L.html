Calculating Section Surface Area: Stylized vs 3D Method

When working with h.Section objects in NEURON, the surface area calculation depends on how the section geometry is specified: the stylized method or the 3D pt3d method.

Area Calculation Using Stylized Geometry

If the section geometry is set using the stylized method by assigning

  • sec.L (length), and

  • sec.diam (diameter)

then the surface area of the segment at midpoint (sec(x).area()) corresponds to the side area of a cylinder (assuming a fixed diameter along its length):

\[\text{Area} \approx L * \pi * \text{diam}\]

This can be computed manually, for example:

from neuron import h
sec = h.Section()
sec.L = 7.0
sec.diam = 35.0
approx_area = sec.L * sec.diam * 3.141592653589793
neuron_area = sec(0.5).area()
print("Custom area:", approx_area)
print("NEURON area:", neuron_area)

Output:

Custom area: 769.690200129
NEURON area: 769.690200129

In this case, both formulas yield the same result.

Area Calculation Using 3D Point Geometry

If the section geometry is defined with the 3D method using h.pt3dadd() to specify points along the section with explicit coordinates and diameters, the area computation is more accurate and accounts for tapering and segment geometry.

Surface area of a segment in this case is calculated by summing the lateral surface areas of all truncated cones (frusta) formed by adjacent 3D points along the section. Each frustum contributes:

\[A_i = \pi (r_i + r_{i+1}) \cdot s_i\]

where

  • (r_i, r_{i+1}) are the radii at adjacent 3D points,

  • (s_i = sqrt{Delta x^2 + Delta y^2 + Delta z^2}) is the length of the segment between points considering 3D distance.

This formula is derived from the lateral surface area of a conical frustum.

Example illustrating the difference:

from neuron import h
sec = h.Section()
h.pt3dadd(0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 60.0, sec=sec)  # x, y, z, diameter
h.pt3dadd(7.0, 0.0, 0.0, 10.0, sec=sec)
print("sec L: ", sec.L)            # length considered along x-axis
print("sec diam: ", sec.diam)      # diameter at midpoint
custom_area = sec.L * sec.diam * 3.141592653589793  # stylized formula ignoring taper
neuron_area = sec(0.5).area()
print("Custom area:", custom_area)
print("NEURON area (frustum-based):", neuron_area)

Output:

sec L:  7.0
sec diam:  35.0
Custom area: 769.690200129
NEURON area (frustum-based): 2854.61711509

Note

The difference arises because sec.diam reports the diameter at the midpoint only, whereas the actual surface area sums contributions from all cross-sectional changes between 3D points.

Note

Even if two 3D points share the same coordinates but have different diameters, a nonzero area contribution exists because of diameter difference over zero length.

Transmembrane Current Computations

NEURON uses the actual surface area calculated by SectionRef.area (the frustum summation if 3D points are used) for all transmembrane current and ion flux computations. This ensures that membrane currents are computed on the physically accurate surface rather than a simplified cylinder approximation.

Summary

  • Stylized geometry (setting L and diam) leads to area approximated as length × diameter × π.

  • 3D geometry (using pt3dadd()) leads to area computed as sum of conical frusta lateral areas, taking tapering and spatial changes into account.

  • The method sec(x).area() returns the correct membrane surface area at position x along the section.

  • NEURON uses this computed area internally for transmembrane currents.
