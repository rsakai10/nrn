Sections Connectivity and Electrical Properties

Sections in NEURON models are connected end-to-end via their 0 and 1 ends, with the axial resistance (\(R_a\)) representing the resistor between segments. The equivalent circuit of connected sections resembles cylindrical cable segments joined by axial resistors as shown in Section diagrams (cf. The NEURON Book, Fig. 5.6D).

Note

Total membrane capacitance is the integral of capacitance over the entire cell surface and can be calculated by summing capacitances of all segments across sections. However, this does not imply that membrane capacitances or resistances are simply in parallel, because finite axial resistivity separates membrane patches.

Input Resistance and Membrane Resistance

  • The input resistance (\(R_{in}\)) is location-dependent and cannot be computed by directly combining resistances of individual sections with parallel or series formulas due to complex morphology and axial resistance.

  • Membrane resistance of an isolated compartment with only passive conductance (\(g_{pas}\)) can be approximated as

    rm = 1.0 / h.g_pas[seg.x]  # specific membrane resistance (ohm cm²)
Rin_compartment = rm / seg.area()  # Input resistance approximation for that compartment

  • For the entire model or complex cells, measure Rin() by applying current and measuring steady-state voltage:

    stim.amp = 0.1  # nA
h.finitialize(-65)
h.continuerun(100)
Rin = (v_final - v_rest) / stim.amp  # Ohm's law

Membrane Time Constant (\(\tau_m\))

  • Defined as \(\tau_m = R_m C_m\) using specific membrane resistance (\(R_m\), ohm cm²) and specific membrane capacitance (\(C_m\), F/cm²), giving units of time (s).

  • Because increasing surface area proportionally increases capacitance and decreases resistance, \(\tau_m\) is independent of membrane area.

  • In multi-compartment models, \(\tau_m\) for the whole neuron is not a single value but a combination of exponentials reflecting cable properties. Numeric simulation and exponential fitting are needed rather than direct calculation.
